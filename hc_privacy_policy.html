Privacy Policy

Kashkool — Hand Rummy Scorer (كشكول الهاند)

Effective date: July 24, 2026
Developer: Ahmed M. Abed
Contact: aabed91@gmail.com
Applies to: the "Kashkool" mobile application for Android (ama.app.hand_calculator) and iOS (ama.app.handCalculator), referred to below as the "App".

1. Overview

Kashkool is a score-keeping utility for the Hand Rummy card game. We designed the App around a simple principle: your data stays on your device. The App does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or phone number, and does not transmit your game data anywhere.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is created by you and stored only locally on your device:

  • Player names, emojis, and groups you create;
  • Game scores, rounds, results, and match history;
  • App preferences (language, theme, display settings).

This information never leaves your device through the App. We have no servers and no access to it. Deleting the App (or clearing its data on Android) permanently removes all of it.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App integrates a small number of third-party services that are standard for free mobile applications. These services may automatically collect limited technical information as described below.

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS, where permitted);
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it;
  • Device and network information (model, OS version, carrier);
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks).

This collection is governed by Google's own policies: see Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android: "Delete advertising ID"; iOS: "Allow Apps to Request to Track" / Limit Ad Tracking).

Removing ads: the App offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. After this purchase, no ads are shown and the AdMob SDK no longer serves ad content.

3.2 Purchases — Google Play Billing / Apple App Store

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. Transactions are subject to the Google privacy policy or the Apple privacy policy respectively.

3.3 Sharing Results

When you use the "Share" feature, the App generates an image or text of your game result and hands it to the app you choose (e.g., WhatsApp). What happens after that is governed by the receiving app's privacy policy. The App itself does not upload the shared content anywhere.

4. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts;
  • We do not access your precise location, camera, microphone, or photos;
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any data;
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that profile you;
  • We do not require registration or login.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The App contains no chat, no social features, and no user-generated content beyond locally stored player names. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service integrated in the App, please contact us and we will assist in addressing it.

6. Data Security

Because your game data is stored only on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, encryption, OS updates). Data collected by third-party services (Section 3) is protected under those providers' security practices.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access / deletion of local data: all App data can be viewed inside the App and permanently deleted by uninstalling the App (iOS/Android) or clearing app data (Android).
  • Ad personalization: can be limited or reset in your device settings, as described in Section 3.1.
  • Ad-free use: available through the "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/GDPR, CCPA), you may have additional rights regarding data processed by Google; please refer to Google's privacy documentation linked above.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time (for example, if we add new features or services). The "Effective date" at the top will always reflect the latest version. Material changes will be noted in the App's store listing update notes.
